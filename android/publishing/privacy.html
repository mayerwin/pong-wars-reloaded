Pong Wars Reloaded
Privacy policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This policy applies to the Pong Wars Reloaded Android TV and Google TV app, including its Google Play and sideload editions.

Data collection and sharing

Pong Wars Reloaded runs offline. The app does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer or third parties. It does not contain advertising, analytics, an account system, online multiplayer, or an in-app purchase system.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your game preferences and controller profiles on the device where you play. A controller profile can include the input device name and identifier supplied by Android, its button or axis mappings, and whether you completed the controls check. This lets your controller rejoin quickly during later sessions.

Button presses, stick positions, and mouse movements are processed locally to control the game. They are not sent off the device. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, or Bluetooth device scanning. Pairing accessories is handled by your TV's system settings.

Retention and deletion

Saved preferences and profiles remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. You can change a player's mapping through the controls option in the pause menu. To remove all saved information, use your TV's Settings > Apps > Pong Wars Reloaded > Clear data, or uninstall the app. The Play and sideload editions keep separate app storage.

The app disables Android cloud backup and excludes its stored data from Android's device-transfer backup rules.

Platform services

Android, Google TV, and Google Play operate independently of the game and may process information under their own policies, for example when downloading an app or updating the system. This policy describes the behavior of Pong Wars Reloaded itself.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact listed on the app's Google Play page. You can also find the project and its support channel at the Pong Wars Reloaded repository. Avoid posting personal information in public issue reports.
